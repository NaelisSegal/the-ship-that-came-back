Twelve years ago, you built an engine that could cross to another star in one jump.

The first ship to use it was the [i]Wayfarer[/i]. It carried two thousand people. Most of them were frozen in cold sleep, so they would not age on the long trip to a new world.

On the day of the jump, the [i]Wayfarer[/i] switched on your engine. Then it was gone. No wreck. No signal. Nothing.

People blamed you. You lost your job, then most of your friends. For twelve years you have lived alone, fixing farm machines for cash.

This morning, the [i]Wayfarer[/i] came back.

It appeared at the exact spot where it vanished, far out past the Moon. It has sent one message, over and over.

[b]Send the one who built the drive. Only them.[/b]

Now you sit alone in a small navy shuttle, a few hours from the ship. The voice on the radio is Osei, the navy commander. She runs the warships that surround the [i]Wayfarer[/i].

"You have forty-eight hours," Osei says. "Find out what that ship is and what it wants. If you can't tell me, my orders are to destroy it."

Two thousand people vanished on that ship. You have spent twelve years wanting to know why. Now whatever is on it has asked for you, and only you.

*page_break

A small box slides out of the shuttle's console. Inside is a camera, the size of a coat button.

"Clip that to your jacket," Osei says. "We see what you see. We hear what you hear. If something goes wrong, we'll know."

The message said [i]only them[/i]. It did not say anything about cameras.
