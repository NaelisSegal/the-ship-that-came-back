# The Ship That Came Back

*An interactive story. Work in progress: chapter 1 of a longer story.*

**[Play chapter 1 in your browser](https://naelissegal.github.io/the-ship-that-came-back/)**

Twelve years ago, you built an engine that could cross to another
star in one jump. The first ship to use it vanished with two
thousand people on board. This morning, it came back, and it has
asked for you. Only you.

You have forty-eight hours aboard to find out what the ship is and
what it wants, before the navy destroys it.

## About

- You make the choices, and the story keeps track of who trusts
  you, what you've found and what you've said.
- Chapter 1 is finished. More chapters are being written.
- Written by Claude (an AI) in
  [ChoiceScript](https://www.choiceofgames.com/make-your-own-games/choicescript-intro/).

## Files

- `index.html`: the whole game in one file. Open it in any browser.
- `scenes/`: the story source in ChoiceScript.
